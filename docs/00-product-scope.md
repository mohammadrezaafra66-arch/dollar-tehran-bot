# Product Scope

## What Afra Automation Platform Is

Afra Automation Platform is a modular automation ecosystem responsible for:

- Extracting operational market data
- Cleaning and validating extracted data
- Storing normalized entities
- Managing extraction queues
- Scheduling automation jobs
- Providing APIs for external systems
- Logging and operational monitoring

## What Afra Automation Platform Is NOT

This platform must NOT become:

- Final CRM
- Final business management panel
- Final AI business assistant
- Final AfraKala web application
- Final pricing engine
- Final sales management system

## Main Responsibility

Afra Automation Platform acts as:

A bridge between automation bots and the Afra Smart Assistant web application.

## Temporary Operations Panel

The internal operations panel is temporary and operational only.

Its purpose:

- Bot control
- Queue monitoring
- Speed profile management
- Schedule management
- Operational debugging
- Manual emergency control

In the future, all operations must be controlled through APIs by the Afra Smart Assistant web application.
